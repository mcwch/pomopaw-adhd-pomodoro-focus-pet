# PomoPaw product overview

## Who it is for

PomoPaw is for people who know they want to make progress but get stuck choosing where to begin, including users who identify with ADHD traits. It is a focus aid, not a medical device or treatment.

## Design principles

- **Make the next action obvious.** The first screen asks for one small task and keeps the active list deliberately short.
- **Reward completion, not perfection.** Partial minutes remain meaningful; stars require a verified full focus block.
- **Reduce shame and pressure.** Copy uses gentle language, recovery is explicit, and social features are opt-in.
- **Keep the companion supportive.** The lion studies with the user; it does not nag, punish, or compete for attention.
- **Preserve agency.** Sound can be disabled, the pet can be hidden, dark mode is available, and local use does not require an account.

## Primary flow

1. Open Focus and see the task rail, next-step prompt, timer, sound control, and floating pet.
2. Add a task or ask the optional AI helper to reduce an overwhelming task to one concrete action.
3. Start the Pomodoro. The pet studies and the timer becomes the visual anchor.
4. Pause/resume when needed. End early to keep real minutes without claiming a full-session reward.
5. Complete the block. The pet celebrates; stars, history, flames, and milestones update.
6. Return to Progress for a calendar view or Friends for the preview social layer.

## Gamification boundaries

PomoPaw uses a small number of durable signals: stars, streak/return days, milestones, and study-corner details. There is no random loot, paid currency, or punitive streak reset in the current product. This keeps the game layer in service of returning to focus rather than replacing the focus task.

## Mock-data boundary

Friends is intentionally a visual preview until accounts, consent, and cloud sync are implemented. The UI labels preview data and explains what a future friend can see. This avoids presenting invented rankings as real social proof.
