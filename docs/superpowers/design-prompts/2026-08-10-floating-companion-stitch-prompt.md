# Stitch prompt: floating study companion

Create a desktop-first Focus Companion web app screen for an ADHD-friendly Pomodoro timer. Keep the existing Stitch visual language and the blue-maned lion studying-at-a-desk illustration as the single companion character.

The lion must be a persistent floating companion, not a large right-side Study Corner card. Anchor it in the lower-right corner above the app shell with a small speech bubble and a compact light halo/stage so it remains readable in both light and dark mode. The companion layer must not block the timer, task list, calendar, or primary controls. Include a small hide/collapse affordance and a subtle drag affordance; the user should be able to reposition it anywhere on the desktop. Keep the character bright and blue in dark mode, with no black color treatment and no white rectangular image tile.

Use these companion states:

- Focus: “Studying with you” and “One small step at a time.”
- Break or pause: “Resting with you” and “A real pause counts too.”
- Completed focus: “Celebrating with you” and “You earned a focus star!”
- Idle/progress: “Ready when you are” or “Keeping your place.”

The main Focus screen should become an open two-column composition: a narrow Today task rail on the left and a dominant timer canvas on the right. The previous large Study Corner panel is removed to create breathing room for the timer, AI task-shrinking helper, sound controls, and future social widgets. The Progress screen should keep the real monthly calendar and metrics as the primary content; replace the large room scene with a compact milestone/friends summary while the floating lion remains globally visible.

Preserve existing interaction hierarchy: Start 25 minutes, Pause/Resume, End early, None/Rain/Café/Forest/White noise, local AI “Help me shrink this into a first step,” streak/verified-minute metrics, and the future Friends entry point. Treat the lion as a supportive study partner, never as a game distraction.
