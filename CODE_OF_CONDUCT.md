# Code of Conduct

PomoPaw follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone. Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and prioritize the safety and dignity of contributors.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the maintainers through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Project maintainers will review reports and respond proportionally and confidentially.
